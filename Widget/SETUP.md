# Life Clock

### Your desktop widget · Quick setup

```text
┌────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐
│ Add widget │ →  │ Set birthday │ →  │ Choose clock │
└────────────┘    └──────────────┘    └──────────────┘
```

## 1 · Add your widget

Open **Life Clock** from Applications once. Right-click your desktop → **Edit Widgets** → search **Life Clock**. Add the small or medium widget.

## 2 · Enter your birthday

Right-click the widget → **Edit Widget**.

- Choose your birth **Day**, **Month** and **Year**.
- Enter your birth time if known, using **HH:mm**, such as **14:30**. Otherwise, leave it blank; the time will be approximate.
- Choose your birth timezone. For India, select **Asia/Kolkata**.

## 3 · Choose your clock

| Clock | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Age** | Time since your birth. |
| **Years remaining** | Time until your chosen target age. |

For **Years remaining**, set **Target age (personal estimate)**. The default is **80**; you can choose **1–150**. This is your personal estimate. The countdown stops at zero.

## 4 · Choose what appears

**Years always stay enabled.** New widgets show months and days by default. Switch **Hours**, **Minutes** and **Seconds** on for more detail. Enable **Seconds** to see the clock tick.

Click outside the settings to finish.

---

**Need help?** If settings look outdated, remove the widget and add it again. Existing widgets keep their saved switches. Each widget has its own birthday settings, separate from the floating desktop card.

Live calendar updates require **macOS 15+**; macOS 14 refreshes periodically. Zero-valued units may disappear.
